Dragon Quest Legends - Overview: Dragon Quest Legends is a fantasy role-playing game developed by Mountain Peak Studios, a game studio based in Kathmandu, Nepal. The game was released in March 2025 and is available on PC, PlayStation 5, and mobile devices
Dragon Quest Legends - Story: The player takes the role of a young hero named Arin, who lives in the village of Eldoria. When ancient dragons begin attacking nearby kingdoms, Arin must travel across the world, collect the five Dragon Stones, and defeat the Dragon King before he destroys the realm.
Dragon Quest Legends - Gameplay: The game uses turn-based combat with a skill tree system. Players explore an open world, complete side quests, craft weapons, and recruit companions. A typical playthrough of the main story takes about 40 hours, and completing every side quest can take over 80 hours.
Dragon Quest Legends - Characters: The main characters are Arin the hero, Lyra the archer, Borak the dwarf warrior, and Mira the healer. Each companion has a unique skill tree and a personal quest line that unlocks special abilities. The final enemy is the Dragon King, Vorgath.
Dragon Quest Legends - Game Modes: Dragon Quest Legends offers a single-player story mode, a 4-player online co-op mode for dungeon raids, and a weekly challenge mode with leaderboards. Hard mode unlocks after finishing the story once.
Dragon Quest Legends - Price and Platforms: The PC version costs 29.99 USD on Steam. The PlayStation 5 version costs 39.99 USD. The mobile version is free to download with optional in-app purchases for cosmetic items. A deluxe edition adds a soundtrack and exclusive armor skins for 49.99 USD.
Dragon Quest Legends - Reviews and Awards: The game received an average critic score of 87 out of 100. Critics praised the art style, the music, and the combat system. The most common complaint was the slow start of the first two hours. It won the Best Indie RPG award at the 2025 South Asia Game Awards.

Neon Racers 2099 - Overview: Neon Racers 2099 is a futuristic arcade racing game developed by Turbo Lotus Games, a studio based in Pokhara, Nepal. It was released in September 2024 for PC, Xbox Series X, and PlayStation 5. The game is set in a cyberpunk city where racers compete on floating tracks above neon-lit streets.
Neon Racers 2099 - Gameplay: Players drive hover cars at speeds above 500 km/h, using boost pads, drift mechanics, and weapons such as magnetic mines and shock waves. There are 24 tracks, 30 cars, and a garage where players upgrade the engine, shield, and handling. The career mode takes around 15 hours to finish.
Neon Racers 2099 - Game Modes: The game includes a career mode, time trials, a split-screen mode for up to 4 players, and an online ranked mode with up to 12 racers per match. A daily race mode gives players a new track and car combination every 24 hours.
Neon Racers 2099 - Price and Platforms: Neon Racers 2099 costs 24.99 USD on PC and 34.99 USD on Xbox Series X and PlayStation 5. A season pass costs 12.99 USD and adds 8 extra tracks and 5 extra cars. The game supports controllers and steering wheels.
Neon Racers 2099 - Reviews and Awards: Neon Racers 2099 has an average critic score of 82 out of 100. Reviewers praised the sense of speed and the synthwave soundtrack. The main criticism was the limited number of online players on weekdays. It was nominated for Best Racing Game at the 2024 Himalayan Game Awards.

Farm Valley Stories - Overview: Farm Valley Stories is a relaxing farming and life simulation game developed by Green Terrace Studio, an independent team from Lalitpur, Nepal. It was released in April 2023 on PC, Nintendo Switch, and mobile. The player inherits an old farm from a grandparent and rebuilds it into a thriving village.
Farm Valley Stories - Gameplay: Players grow crops, raise animals such as cows, chickens, and goats, fish in the river, and sell products at the weekly market. The game has four seasons, and each season lasts 28 in-game days. Players can also befriend 20 villagers, attend festivals, and get married in the village.
Farm Valley Stories - Game Modes: The game offers a peaceful mode with no time pressure and a standard mode with energy limits and seasonal deadlines. A co-op mode lets up to 2 friends share one farm online. There is no combat in the game.
Farm Valley Stories - Price and Platforms: Farm Valley Stories costs 14.99 USD on PC and Nintendo Switch. The mobile version costs 4.99 USD with no in-app purchases or advertisements. A free demo covers the first season and can be played on PC.

Farm Valley Stories - Reviews and Awards: Farm Valley Stories received an average critic score of 90 out of 100 and is one of the best-rated games from Nepal. Critics praised its cozy atmosphere, hand-drawn art, and relaxing music. The most common complaint was that inventory space is too small early in the game. It won the Best Casual Game award at the 2023 South Asia Game Awards.
Galaxy Defenders - Overview: Galaxy Defenders is a science-fiction tower defense game developed by Orbit Forge Studio, based in Bhaktapur, Nepal. It was released in January 2025 for PC and mobile devices. Players protect a space station from waves of alien invaders.

Galaxy Defenders - Gameplay: Players build laser towers, missile launchers, and shield generators on a grid map and earn credits by defeating enemies. There are 40 levels across 5 planets, and each planet ends with a boss fight. Players choose one of three commanders, each with a special ability such as an orbital strike or a freeze pulse.

Galaxy Defenders - Game Modes: The game includes a campaign mode, an endless survival mode where the goal is to last as many waves as possible, and a daily puzzle level. There is no multiplayer mode, but players can compare survival scores on a global leaderboard.

Galaxy Defenders - Price and Platforms: Galaxy Defenders is free to play on mobile with ads that can be removed for 3.99 USD. The PC version costs 9.99 USD on Steam and includes all commanders and planets. There are no paid upgrades that affect gameplay.

Galaxy Defenders - Reviews and Awards: Galaxy Defenders holds an average critic score of 78 out of 100. Reviewers liked the clear tower upgrade system and the boss fights. They disliked the repetitive music and the steep difficulty on planet 4. The game has been downloaded more than 2 million times on mobile.
